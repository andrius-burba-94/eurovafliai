## Final verdict

Ship. Title and phase-specific doors lead; setup alone carries invite, readiness
and order apparatus. Related destinations share one Door. Members and chat use
one framed level with no radius, shadow or nesting.

Exactly one act carries marker at each setup stage: Ready while the viewer is
unready, Roll once everybody is ready and no order exists, then Start once the
order exists. Re-apply and Reshuffle stay ink. Drafting members and both order
runs print their slot numbers in ink.

Lobby chat opens with a ruled transcript and its composer on the lower edge.
The open Hide control lives in the Bank heading; the draft room retains the
folded latest-line state. Populated captures prove the system's dashed rail-blue
voice and the viewer's left-aligned, named line.
