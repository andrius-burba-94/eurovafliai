Cognitive load failed four of eight checks. Empty and correction copy was
honest, but accessing historical scoring required remembering and preserving a
query string that the interface never exposed.

## Findings

- P1: season was copied parse-or-env logic on three pages and absent from the
  transaction builder; cross-page links dropped it.
- P1: season content Banks were still unframed after the U1-U3 rollout.
- P1: selected transaction rows used the on-the-clock marker at the same time
  as the Record action.
- P2: the shared radar became a dead hash destination on the team page, where
  no draft board exists.

## Verdict

No-ship as U4. Provide one shared framed season control, preserve season across
the reading path, frame sibling tasks and keep selection in ink.
