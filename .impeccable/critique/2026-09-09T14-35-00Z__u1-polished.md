## Combined verdict

The framed material produces programme-like task groups without nesting, radius
or elevation. Setup rows are waiting, established leagues are filled, and the
clock's marker is not borrowed for a drafting status. A populated home demotes
Create to ink; an empty home still gives its only creation path the marker.

The visible roster line now says whose counts they are and prints current over
template totals. Its patches expose full accessible names while hiding the
abbreviated visual figures. Rail controls encode and measure at least 44px on
both axes.

Cognitive load has one failure, down from three: Start and Join remain visible
for returning members. That is accepted because the U1 brief explicitly keeps
the two framed doors rather than adding disclosure machinery.
