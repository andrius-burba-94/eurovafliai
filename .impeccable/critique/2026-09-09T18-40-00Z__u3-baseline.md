Scope: pre-U3 draft and narrowed-pool captures at desktop and Pixel 7 widths.

| **Total** |  | **27/40** |

Cognitive load failed five of eight checks. The room's state language and
choose-then-confirm behavior were strong; grouping and movement between its two
roster views were not.

## Findings

- P1: pool, radar and board ran together on stock while simpler product
  surfaces already used the framed task material.
- P1: `You still need` was an orphan below the sticky band, so it left the
  viewport as soon as the viewer entered the pool.
- P1: the radar could name a roster but could not move the horizontally
  scrolling board to that member's column.
- P1 outside U3's accepted behavior scope: commissioner and personal controls
  keep the first pool row below the fold on a phone.
- P2: the desktop keyboard sentence used slot-label caps and tracking.

## Verdict

No-ship as U3. Preserve the server-authoritative tap path, sticky blush and 2px
rule; add grouping, persistent needs and a real radar-to-column route without
changing how a pick lands.
