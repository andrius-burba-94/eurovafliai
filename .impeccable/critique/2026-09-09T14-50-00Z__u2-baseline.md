# U2 baseline critique — lobby and chat

Scope: the pre-U2 drafting lobby at desktop and Pixel 7 widths.

## Design health

| # | Heuristic | Score |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Visibility of system status | 2 |
| 2 | Match with the real world | 3 |
| 3 | User control and freedom | 3 |
| 4 | Consistency and standards | 2 |
| 5 | Error prevention | 3 |
| 6 | Recognition rather than recall | 2 |
| 7 | Flexibility and efficiency | 2 |
| 8 | Aesthetic and minimalist design | 2 |
| 9 | Error recovery | 3 |
| 10 | Help and documentation | 2 |
| **Total** |  | **24/40** |

Cognitive load failed five of eight checks. The board language, stock, rules,
tabular figures and single prominent room entry were strong. The state and task
order were not.

## Findings

- P0: the drafting page still printed setup-only instructions to Manage members
  and roll an order, even though those controls no longer existed.
- P1: the live door preceded the page title and used `slot-live`, the material
  reserved for an on-clock slot.
- P1: Members and chat did not use the approved framed material. The ready tally
  survived after setup, while member rows did not carry readiness on their rule.
- P1: nine free slots and an empty BoardPlan survived after kickoff.
- P1: chat was a collapsed Show row rather than a framed transcript with its
  composer attached to the lower edge.
- P1: the four route rows repeated their title, copy, verb and touch-target
  markup rather than sharing one `Door`.

## Verdict

No-ship as U2. The baseline was a coherent prior lobby, but it contradicted the
current phase and did not meet the panel, order, readiness or chat acceptance
criteria.
